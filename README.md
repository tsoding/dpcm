# DPCM

Notes from the [1-bit Music in C](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GISS1coMV8c) session.

## Quick Start

> [!WARNING]
> This is an audio programming project! Plug in your headphones, but do not put them on until your make sure that the sound levels produced by the programs are safe!

```console
$ cc -o nob nob.c
$ ./nob -run -dpcm1 -play
```

*The music sample is by [nu11](https://nu11.bandcamp.com/).*
